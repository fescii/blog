My work focuses heavily on systems engineering, building custom infrastructure from scratch rather than relying on standard third-party tools. Below is an overview of my active projects, spanning operating systems, mobility platforms, and underlying data infrastructure.

### Platform OS
An operating system designed around web-like discovery. The architecture focuses on on-device AI implementation utilizing WASM-style sandboxing and NPU passthrough. It is engineered specifically for discovery rather than functioning as a standard web replacer.

### ATOM Framework
A custom, multi-timeline transcription format. The system is built entirely on pure algorithms and system-level implementations in Rust and C. It is designed from the ground up to ensure high-performance processing without the overhead of Python.

### Vouch
The underlying technical infrastructure for a trust-based mobility platform operating in the Kenyan market. The system handles trust scoring, community endorsement, OTP verification, and voice bridging for the local transport ecosystem.

### aduki, Inc. Infrastructure
As co-founder, I architect and scale our core technologies and internal labs. Our active suite of high-performance tools includes:

*   **Nendi:** Infrastructure for efficient change data capture.
*   **Sando:** Systems for high-performance media processing.
*   **Colink:** Protocols for distributed network connectivity.

### Ecosystem & Market Platforms

*   **Diasync Health:** Technical architecture for a concierge medical service facilitating on-site care and emergency evacuation.
*   **Syncs Connect:** Development of a skill economy marketplace platform.
